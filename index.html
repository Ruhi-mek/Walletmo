What is Walletmo?

Walletmo is a modern personal finance app designed to help young people take control of their money in a simple and understandable way. It allows users to track their income and expenses, create budgets, set savings goals, monitor their spending habits, and receive useful financial insights. With a clean and user-friendly dashboard, Walletmo turns financial information into clear visuals and actionable insights, helping users build better money habits and work toward their financial goals.
